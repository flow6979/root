## Full description (draft)
Root is a gentle companion for anyone stuck in the late-night scroll, the endless
Reels, the "five more minutes" that becomes two hours.

Instead of shaming you, Root catches the moment - the instant you open a time-sink app,
it steps in like a friend and offers a pause. No nagging, no guilt. Just a calmer choice.

- A friend to talk to: short, warm AI reflection sessions when your head is noisy.
- Shield: a gentle pause when you open the apps that pull you in, with insight into
  where your time really goes.
- Moments: notice the small real-life choices - meals, sleep, energy.
- Stories: a finite, calming read that ends on purpose, so the scroll doesn't own you.
- Your day, your colour: the whole app follows the sky - or switch to minimalist black
  and white.

Root asks for only what it needs, keeps your private reflections private, and never
uses your camera or microphone in the background.

Free to use. Optional premium unlocks stricter blocking, deeper sessions, and insights.
