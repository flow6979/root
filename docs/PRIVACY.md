# Root - Privacy Policy (draft)

_Last updated: 2026-07-27. This is a draft to host at a public URL (e.g. GitHub Pages)
before the Play Store listing. Have it reviewed before publishing._

Root ("we", "the app") helps you build healthier digital habits. We designed Root to
collect as little as possible and to keep your most personal content private.

## What Root accesses (all with your permission)
- **App usage** (Usage Access): to detect when you open a monitored app and show a
  gentle pause. Usage data is processed on your device.
- **Display over other apps**: to show the pause screen. No data is collected by this.
- **Notifications**: to run the background protection and check in with you.
- **What you type in reflection sessions**: sent to our AI provider to generate replies.
- **Mood check-ins and meals you log**: stored to show your progress.

## Where your data goes
- **On your device**: mood, streak, meals, and app-usage stats are stored locally.
- **Cloud (Supabase)**: if you use an account, your mood/meal/reflection records sync to
  our database, protected by row-level security so only your account can read them.
- **AI provider (Groq)**: reflection messages are sent to generate the friend's replies.
  We chose a provider that states it does not train on your data.

## What we do NOT do
- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use camera or microphone in the background.
- Our product analytics are aggregate and event-based only - we never send the *content*
  of your reflections or moods to analytics.

## Your controls
- You can revoke any permission in Android Settings at any time.
- You can request deletion of your account data by contacting us.
